Ceremony Music Guide

Indie Wedding Music

Alternative, romantic, and timeless music ideas for each moment of your wedding ceremony.

Choosing your ceremony music is about creating a natural emotional arc—from the quiet anticipation as guests arrive to the joyful moment you walk back up the aisle as newlyweds. Below are the main musical phases of a wedding ceremony, with both alternative/indie selections and more traditional wedding choices for each.
1

Prelude — As Guests Arrive

Prelude music is played while guests are arriving and being seated, usually for 15–30 minutes before the ceremony begins. The music should feel warm, romantic, and welcoming without being overly dramatic.

Modern & Indie Options Traditional Options
Anchor — Novo Amor Air on the G String — J.S. Bach
Bloom — The Paper Kites Jesu, Joy of Man’s Desiring — J.S. Bach
Heartbeats — José González Clair de Lune — Claude Debussy
Mystery of Love — Sufjan Stevens The Swan — Camille Saint-Saëns
Skinny Love — Bon Iver Gymnopédie No. 1 — Erik Satie
Young & Beautiful — Lana Del Rey Minuet in G — Christian Petzold
No One’s Gonna Love You — Band of Horses Prelude in C Major, BWV 846 — J.S. Bach
Lovesong — The Cure Largo from Xerxes — Handel
Maps — Yeah Yeah Yeahs Flower Duet from Lakmé — Léo Delibes
2

Wedding Party Processional

This music accompanies the entrance of parents, grandparents, bridesmaids, groomsmen, and/or other members of the wedding party. These selections can gradually build anticipation while leaving room for the bride’s entrance to have its own special musical moment.

Modern & Indie Options Traditional Options
Falling Slowly — Glen Hansard & Markéta Irglová Canon in D — Pachelbel
First Day of My Life — Bright Eyes Arioso — J.S. Bach
Turning Page — Sleeping at Last Sheep May Safely Graze — J.S. Bach
3

Bridal Processional — Walking Down the Aisle

The bride’s entrance is often the musical highlight of the ceremony. Slower, expressive selections work especially well here and help make the walk down the aisle feel distinct and memorable.

Modern & Indie Options Traditional Options
A Thousand Years — Christina Perri Bridal Chorus (“Here Comes the Bride”) — Wagner
Flightless Bird, American Mouth — Iron & Wine Trumpet Voluntary — Jeremiah Clarke
4

Ceremony Interlude

Some ceremonies include music during a unity candle, communion, signing of the marriage license, prayer, remembrance, or another meaningful pause.

Modern & Indie Options Traditional Options
There Is a Light That Never Goes Out — The Smiths Ave Maria — Schubert
Starlight — Muse Meditation from Thaïs — Jules Massenet
5

Recessional — The Newlyweds’ Exit

Once the couple is pronounced married, the recessional should feel joyful, energetic, and celebratory. This is usually the brightest and most upbeat musical moment of the ceremony.

Modern & Indie Options Traditional Options
Ho Hey — The Lumineers Wedding March — Felix Mendelssohn
Home — Edward Sharpe & The Magnetic Zeros Ode to Joy — Beethoven
Such Great Heights — The Postal Service Hornpipe from Water Music — Handel
